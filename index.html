Loading an old board

  1. If you load an old board, you have to recalibrate your position. Count the rows from the bottom up, then set your position on the overlay or in the helper.
  2. Align the board from the bottom row up, until the 5th (top) row is lined up too: every gold outline needs a tile in it, and the top row of outlines must sit on the board's top edge. Your piece (the pin) must sit in the cyan "your piece" outline. If the pin is in another row, or the top outlines are over empty space, the board is a row off.
  3. After that, the board should update by itself (or use the Manually take a picture button), with most tiles set up correctly.
  4. Worst case, set up the tiles yourself, and it will work from there.

How it thinks

Rarity points: what one chest in a slot is worth to you (same loot pool for every chest, better odds the rarer it is). Press Reset for the current defaults.

On the game

The app draws these over the game. The marks can't be clicked; drag a guide by its top bar to move it (it stays where you put it), × hides it. Hotkeys for each are in the Hotkeys tab.

Board

Values for "Highest average value": green 1, blue 1.2, purple 4, gold 10, red 16, rainbow 25. Upgrade cards hit your highest chest they're allowed to touch. 7 chest slots, 10 cards in hand. At 0 life the board ends and you keep your chests.

Board logs

What is kept: only the game's own lines about the Tarot Path board (a scrubber drops chat, friends, account, ids, network and system lines before they are written), the pictures of the game window after each move, the reader's flags and your debug marks. Pictures are of the game window only, never the desktop. Sent anonymously (no name unless you add one; the collector doesn't keep your IP address). Boards you don't send stay on this PC in companion\sessions.

Help make the Arcana Helper better?

You can send the board logs from your games to the helper's owner. Every board teaches the screen reader to read boards more accurately and faster, so the helper fills in more of the board by itself and you fix less, for everyone who uses it.

  • Only the Tarot Path board. Before anything is saved, a scrubber keeps just the game's own lines about the board (tiles, moves, cards, chests) and drops everything else: chat, friends, your account, IDs, network and system lines.
  • Pictures of the game window only, taken after each move. Never your desktop or any other program.
  • Anonymous. No account, and no name unless you add one below. The collector doesn't keep your IP address. Only your Windows version goes along, to spot PC-specific problems.
  • Only from now on. Boards you played before stay on this PC. Logs go a few boards at a time, at most 8 uploads a day.
  • You're in control. Turn it off any time in Settings → Board logs, where you can also open the board logs folder and see exactly what's kept.
Bug reports (your report hotkey) work the same either way.
